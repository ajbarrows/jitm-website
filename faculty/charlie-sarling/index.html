Big Band Director

Charlie Sarling

Trumpet

Biography

Charlie Sarling taught music for 38 years in New York's Brentwood School District, then taught trumpet as an adjunct at Suffolk County Community College until 2018. From 2012 to 2019 he co-coordinated jazz for SCMEA, the Suffolk County Music Educators' Association. He has also been a summer clinician at adult jazz camps in Vermont and Rhode Island.
