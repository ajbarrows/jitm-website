Camp Executive and Big Band Director

Rich Davidian

Woodwinds

  • Camp administration
  • Co-owner, BDI Enterprises

Biography

Rich has played woodwind instruments in all genres of music and shared the stage with many talented Vermont musicians as well as an array of national talent ranging from Big Joe Burrell and Ray Vega, to The Temptations, Eddie Palmieri, and The Who. Rich has performed with numerous Vermont bands and was a charter member and the Music Director of VT's premier big band, the Vermont Jazz Ensemble (1976–2026) producing and directing three of its CD projects as well as hundreds of events and concerts.

In 2010, Rich retired from a 40 year career teaching instrumental music, and still teaches privately, at summer music camps, and district music festivals. He was also instrumental in the creation of the Vermont Association for Jazz Education and has received special recognition from the Vermont Music Educators Association. Rich is currently on the board of the non-profit Vermont Jazz Foundation. Rich also ran an audio recording business, Stolen Moments Audio, doing live sound reinforcement & recording for musical & community groups.

He lives in East Montpelier with his wife Shawn and has two grown sons (also musicians).
