Camp Administrator and Big Band Director

Rich Davidian

Woodwinds

Biography

Rich Davidian grew up in Dearborn, Michigan, earned a music education degree from Wayne State University, and moved to Vermont in 1972. He taught instrumental music in the Montpelier Public Schools for 38 years before retiring in 2010. A charter member of the Vermont Jazz Ensemble, he has been its music director since 1976. He has also run Stolen Moments Audio, a recording and sound reinforcement business, since 1998. He lives in East Montpelier and is a partner in BDI Enterprises, which runs the camp.
