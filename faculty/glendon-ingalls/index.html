Nonet Director

Glendon Ingalls

Trumpet, double bass, electric bass and sousaphone

  • Camp administration
  • Co-owner, BDI Enterprises

Biography

Glendon Ingalls grew up in Johnson, Vermont, and graduated in music from the University of Vermont. A retired public school music educator, he is a two- time past president of the Vermont Association for Jazz Education. He has taught at the UVM Summer Session, Vermont Summer Jazz Camp and Jazz Vermont, and he founded the Rutland Town School Jazz Camp. He teaches privately at Castleton University, Middlebury College and the Middlebury Community Music Center. He also runs Ingalls Music Studio in Rutland and has led the Red Clover Jazz Trio in a weekly gig since 2015. He is a partner in BDI Enterprises, which runs the camp.
