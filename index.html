The camp

Rehearse all week. Perform Thursday night.

Jazz in the Mountains is a week-long camp in Vermont for adult instrumentalists, music educators and working jazz musicians. You'll join an ensemble that rehearses all week and plays the Thursday evening performance, with coaching, improvisation and plenty of time with other players along the way.

The week covers ensemble sound, jazz interpretation and style, improvisation, technique, repertoire and stage presence. Camp is held at the Killington Grand Resort Hotel, and your lodging, breakfasts and dinners are included.

What we value

Built around the music and the people

  1. Growth first

    Musical growth comes ahead of polishing a performance.

  2. Every camper counts

    A high-quality week for every player, whatever their level.

  3. Players helping players

    Campers of mixed abilities support one another.

  4. Room to improvise

    Plenty of chances to take a solo and try new ideas.

  5. Good company

    A welcoming social atmosphere, on and off the bandstand.

  6. Great teachers

    Faculty chosen for their musicianship, people skills and teaching.
