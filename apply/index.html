Apply

Applying takes two steps, an online application and a video audition. Placement decisions go out by April 1.

How to apply

Four steps to the bandstand

  1. 01

    Submit the online application

    Due March 1

    Tell us about yourself, your instrument and your playing experience.

    Complete the application →
  2. 02

    Record and upload your video audition

    Due March 16

    All new applicants must submit a video, and returning campers are encouraged to send a new one. Acceptance and band placement depend on your video, or on the one we already have on file. See the audition instructions below.

    Upload your video →
  3. 03

    Get your placement

    Due April 1

    We'll email you with your acceptance, your band placement and an outline of the week.

  4. 04

    Confirm and pay

    Due May 1

    Confirm that you're attending and request a room type by April 20, then pay the full camp fee by May 1. Ensemble music goes out by July 1, and any gear rentals must be arranged and paid for by July 10.

Who can apply

  • Adults 18 and over
  • Intermediate or advanced instrumentalists
  • Clarinet and flute players must also play saxophone
  • We don't accept vocalists at this time

Video audition

Recording your audition

Download the sheet music and play-along tracks, then follow the instructions for your instrument.

Sheet music and recordingsUpload your video
Recording tips
  1. A smartphone or tablet works fine. Ask a friend to hold it if that helps.
  2. Record in a room without echo, such as a carpeted bedroom.
  3. Light your face well, and keep at least part of your instrument in the frame.
Horns

Everyone uses the same play-along track.

  • Chorus 1: play the head
  • Chorus 2: improvise (optional, but recommended)
  • Chorus 3: play the head

Lead trumpet, lead trombone and bass trombone candidates: also play the lead or bass excerpt from the PDF unaccompanied, without counting the rests.

Piano, guitar and bass

Use the track for your instrument. Chords are removed from the piano and guitar track, and bass is removed from the bass track.

  • Chorus 1: head
  • Chorus 2: accompany
  • Chorus 3: improvise (optional, but recommended)
  • Chorus 4: head
Drums
  • Chorus 1: play the melody's rhythms
  • Chorus 2: comp behind the recorded soloist
  • Chorus 3: trade 8s
  • Chorus 4: comp through the tagged ending

Then play a short sampling of the styles below.

Naming and uploading your video

Name your file with your name, instrument and the camp year, like this:

FirstnameLastname_Instrument_JITM2027.mp4

You may send more than one video. Saxophonists can send a single video and list any other woodwinds they play.
