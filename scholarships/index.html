Scholarships

Every musician belongs here.

Our commitment

We believe every musician deserves a welcome, and that anyone can grow with good teaching and encouragement. Cost shouldn't be what keeps you away.

Jazz in the Mountains sets aside money each year for partial and full scholarships, and private donors add to the fund. Every applicant still auditions to the same musical standard.

Apply for a scholarship

Submit the regular camp application and video audition first, then fill out the scholarship application.

The scholarship application will be posted here soon. Until then, email admin@jazzinthemountains.com.

Support the fund

Your gift helps another musician spend a week making music in the mountains. Gifts of any size are welcome.

Online giving is coming soon. To give now, email admin@jazzinthemountains.com.
