sections:
  - title: About camp
    questions:
      - q: I'm not sure I'm good enough. Should I apply?
        a: >-
          If you play at an intermediate level or above and read music, yes.
          Bands are placed by audition, so you'll play with a well-balanced
          group of musicians. Many first-timers arrive nervous and leave saying
          it was one of the best weeks of their lives.
        testimonial: Hilary Kissel
      - q: Do I have to improvise?
        a: >-
          No, but you'll have chances to try. Solos are encouraged but not
          required, and directors coach improvisation at every level.
      - q: Which instruments do you accept?
        a: >-
          Saxophone, trumpet, trombone, piano, guitar, bass and drums. Clarinet
          and flute players must also play saxophone. We don't accept vocalists
          at this time.
      - q: Is there an age limit?
        a: >-
          Campers must be 21 or older. There's no upper limit, and many of our
          campers are retired.
      - q: I'm a music teacher. Can I earn credit?
        a: >-
          Yes. Graduate and continuing-education credit is available through
          Vermont State University's Jazz Immersion course. Contact Glendon
          Ingalls at
          [ensembles@jazzinthemountains.com](mailto:ensembles@jazzinthemountains.com).
          See [Earn College Credit](/earn-college-credit/) for details.

  - title: Applying
    questions:
      - q: How do I apply?
        a: >-
          Submit the [online application]({{links.forms.application}}) by
          {{deadline.application}}, then [upload your video]({{links.forms.videoUpload}})
          by {{deadline.audition}}. We'll email you about your acceptance and
          band placement by {{deadline.placement}}. See [Apply](/apply/) for
          the full steps.
      - q: I'm a returning camper. Do I need a new video?
        a: >-
          It isn't required, but we strongly encourage it. Otherwise we use the
          one on file.
      - q: Can I request to play in a particular group or with a friend?
        a: We encourage all campers to be open to a range of experiences.
      - q: Is financial help available?
        a: >-
          Yes, we have a limited number of partial and full scholarships. Apply
          to camp first, then submit the scholarship application. Everyone
          auditions to the same standard. See [Scholarships](/scholarships/).

  - title: Before camp
    questions:
      - q: When do I get the music?
        a: >-
          Some of the music for your ensemble goes out by {{deadline.music}}, so
          you have about five weeks for some preparation.
      - q: What should I bring?
        a: >-
          Your instrument(s), instrument stand, mutes, extra reeds, pencil,
          Scotch tape, amp, cables, power cords, and stool (if you require
          something special). We provide music stands and chairs.
      - q: Can I rent gear?
        a: Yes. Arrange and pay for rentals by {{deadline.gear}}.
      - q: How do I get there?
        a: >-
          Camp is at the {{camp.venue}} in {{camp.town}}. If you're flying,
          you'll need a rental car from the airport. Airport options include
          Burlington (1.5–2 hours' drive), Albany (2.25 hours) or Boston
          (2.5–3 hours).

  - title: At camp
    questions:
      - q: What's included in the fee?
        a: >-
          Your room, breakfasts and dinners, hors d'oeuvres at the opening jam
          session and after the concert, tuition and camp amenities. Travel,
          lunches, extra nights, private lessons, gratuities and incidentals
          aren't included.
      - q: Where do I eat lunch?
        a: >-
          If you select a room with a kitchenette, you can bring food for your
          own lunches. There is a lobby café in the hotel with some snack,
          sandwich and salad options. The town of Killington offers bakeries,
          taverns and restaurants.
      - q: Can I bring a partner who isn't playing?
        a: >-
          Yes. Add ${{fees.partner.price}} to the single-room price. That
          includes breakfasts and dinners, the opening jam session and
          post-concert hors d'oeuvres, and the evening performances. See
          [For partners and spouses](/experience/#partners) for things to do
          nearby.
      - q: Can I arrive early or stay late?
        a: Yes, at your own cost.
      - q: Is the hotel accessible?
        a: >-
          The hotel offers accessible rooms, and the ground-floor rehearsal and
          meal spaces are accessible by elevator.
      - q: Can my family watch the performance?
        a: >-
          Yes. The Thursday concert is livestreamed. The link will be on our
          website.
